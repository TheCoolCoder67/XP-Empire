# XP-Empire
Collect materials and sell them for XP. Spend XP for upgrades to earn more XP!
When you spawn, you are in a mine where you click to collect materials.
To move your character, use the WASD keys.
If you want to collect materials, make sure your player is close.
Move to the right or left of the screen to discover new places.
If you touch the person that says **SELL THINGS HERE** it will prompt you to sell something.
Type in the materials ID in your backpack or type in all to sell all of your materials.
